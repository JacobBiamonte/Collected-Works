Method

Lean certification

The aim is to formalize the collection’s mathematical results in Lean, a proof assistant for computer-checked mathematics. Each completed formalization will be linked to its original result and a reproducible build.

01

The original result

Identify the paper, its authors and the exact statement being formalized. Record the assumptions and the earlier results on which its proof depends.

02

The Lean statement

Express the definitions, assumptions and conclusion in Lean. Review the formal statement alongside the original mathematics to check that they agree.

03

The formal proof

Write a proof that Lean checks. A reproducible build and axiom audit must show no sorry or unproved new axioms.

04

The published formalization

Link the proof code, Lean version, dependencies and build instructions to the result in the collection, so others can inspect and check it.

Tracking progress result by result

The goal is to formalize every mathematical result that can be expressed in Lean. The result index will distinguish work in preparation from completed, checked proofs. Computational and experimental findings will have their own reproduction records.
