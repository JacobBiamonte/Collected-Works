About the project

A coherent account across disciplines.

The Biamonte Collected Works is intended to make a distributed body of research readable as one connected mathematical program, and to produce a source-mapped Lean-certified counterpart for every formalizable mathematical result, while preserving the identity, authorship and history of every contributing work.

Why a collected edition

Results developed across Boolean methods, tensor networks, Hamiltonian models, quantum dynamics and learning often illuminate one another. The collection reorganizes them around shared mathematical questions rather than publication chronology.

That editorial connection does not merge distinct papers, erase coauthors or manufacture proof dependencies. Sources and attributions remain visible wherever a result enters the exposition.

Why the evidence layer matters

One central purpose is cumulative formal verification: result by result, the collection aims for source-mapped Lean declarations that build reproducibly without sorry or unproved new axioms. Empirical and computational claims retain their own reproduction evidence.

The internal validation record is not published wholesale. Reader-facing summaries are exported only after review, keeping private sources and operational records outside the public artifact.

Public beta

The architecture, navigation and publication pipeline are active. The works and results indexes are intentionally empty while the first public records are prepared.
