About the project

About the collection.

The project has two goals: to present Jacob Biamonte’s research program as a coherent body of work, and to formalize its mathematical results in Lean.

A collected edition

The three volumes bring together work on Hamiltonian quantum logic and tensor networks, quantum and stochastic dynamics, and algorithms and statistical inference. A unified exposition will explain the results and the mathematical connections between them.

Each result will be linked to its original paper, with full attribution to its authors. The edition is planned for online reading, individual volume PDFs and a single collected PDF, with a shared BibTeX bibliography.

Formal proofs in Lean

Lean is a proof assistant for writing mathematical statements and proofs that a computer can check. The goal is to give the collection’s mathematical results formal proofs, with explicit assumptions and links to the original statements.

The result index will show the progress of each formalization and provide access to completed proofs and reproducible builds.

A foundation for further research

The collected edition and its structured result index will also support the exploration of new connections and extensions, including research assisted by frontier models.
