About the project

A coherent account across disciplines.

The Biamonte Collected Works has two connected purposes: to produce a source-mapped Lean-certified counterpart for every formalizable mathematical result, and to build a structured result database through which researchers and frontier models can explore new connections and extensions.

Why a collected edition

Results developed across Boolean methods, tensor networks, Hamiltonian models, quantum dynamics and learning often illuminate one another. The collection reorganizes them around shared mathematical questions rather than publication chronology.

That editorial connection does not merge distinct papers, erase coauthors or manufacture proof dependencies. Sources and attributions remain visible wherever a result enters the exposition.

Why a result database

Papers are the source record; individual results are the reusable research objects. Structured statements, assumptions, dependencies, topics and evidence states allow ideas to be compared across works and disciplines.

Frontier models can use that structure to propose relationships, conjectures and extensions. Model-generated proposals remain research candidates—not established results—until they pass source review, mathematical checking and the appropriate certification or reproduction track.

Proof and discovery reinforce one another

Formalization makes results dependable units for comparison. New connections and extensions feed back into the same review and certification process, allowing the corpus to grow without blurring established results, reproduced claims and open conjectures.
