Jacob Biamonte · Collected Works

One body of work.
Built for proof and discovery.

The Biamonte Collected Works organizes results across Hamiltonian quantum logic, tensor networks, quantum and stochastic dynamics, algorithms and statistical inference into a coherent research corpus. Its two central aims are to certify every formalizable mathematical result in Lean and to make the result structure usable for new research.

Each result record will expose its statement, assumptions, dependencies, source and certification state. This gives researchers and frontier models precise objects with which to explore connections, conjectures and extensions.

Formal objective

A Lean-certified mathematical record, result by result.

  1. 01
    Identify the source

    Bind a claim to its exact version, authors and locator.

  2. 02
    Check the mathematics

    Separate analytic proof, finite corroboration and unresolved dependencies.

  3. 03
    Reproduce what can be reproduced

    Preserve code, data assumptions and negative controls.

  4. 04
    Certify in Lean

    Map each formalizable result to a declaration, then publish its reproducible build and axiom status.

Discovery objective

Turn the result graph into a research instrument.

The database is designed for frontier-model-assisted searches across statements, assumptions, methods and dependencies: finding analogies, exposing hidden connections and proposing extensions across the three volumes. Those proposals remain candidates until they pass mathematical review, source alignment and, where formalizable, Lean certification.
