The Biamonte Collected Works brings results across Hamiltonian quantum logic, tensor networks, quantum and stochastic dynamics, algorithms and statistical inference into one coherent source. A central goal is to give every formalizable mathematical result a source-mapped Lean certificate.

Certification proceeds result by result: exact statement, explicit assumptions, reproducible Lean build and axiom audit. Empirical and computational claims remain on a separate reproduction track.

Formal objective

A Lean-certified mathematical record, result by result.

  1. 01
    Identify the source

    Bind a claim to its exact version, authors and locator.

  2. 02
    Check the mathematics

    Separate analytic proof, finite corroboration and unresolved dependencies.

  3. 03
    Reproduce what can be reproduced

    Preserve code, data assumptions and negative controls.

  4. 04
    Certify in Lean

    Map each formalizable result to a declaration, then publish its reproducible build and axiom status.
