Reading architecture

Three movements through the research.

The collection follows conceptual dependencies rather than publication chronology. Each result has one primary home, while cross-references preserve the larger network.

{{VOLUME_CARDS}}

Evidence before labels

Certification is a ladder, not a badge.

  1. 01
    Identify the source

    Bind a claim to its exact version, authors and locator.

  2. 02
    Check the mathematics

    Separate analytic proof, finite corroboration and unresolved dependencies.

  3. 03
    Reproduce what can be reproduced

    Preserve code, data assumptions and negative controls.

  4. 04
    Formalize exact statements

    Map results to Lean declarations and report build and axiom status precisely.

What comes next
